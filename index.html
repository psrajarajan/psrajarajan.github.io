About S. Rajarajan

A unique convergence of heavy engineering systems, custom software development, and math visualization.

Hello! I am S. Rajarajan, an educator and developer on a mission to simplify mathematics. I graduated with a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) degree in Physics (1973-76) from Sacred Heart College, Tirupathur, and went on to complete a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Instrument Technology (1976-79) from the prestigious Madras Institute of Technology (MIT), Chennai, India.

For nearly four decades (1979-2016), I worked globally as an engineering professional in Fertilizer, Oil & Gas, and Control & Automation Engineering Industries. This career allowed me to design and troubleshoot highly complex real-time safety systems across India, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Brunei, and Kazakhstan.

Throughout my engineering career, my true passion was teaching Mathematics and Computer Science to high school students. Since retiring in 2016, I have dedicated myself fully to educating, designing software tools, and conducting intensive Class 10 board prep workshops that empower students to secure maximum exam scores through visualization and analytical logic.
