About S. Rajarajan

A unique convergence of engineering, software development, and mathematical storytelling.

My life's work is driven by a profound passion for teaching **Mathematics and Computer Science** to high school students. I believe math shouldn't simply be memorized—it should be visualized, manipulated, and discovered.

Since 2016, I have dedicated myself fully to educating, designing tools, and conducting intensive **one-day board prep revision seminars** to empower Class 10 students to secure top marks on standard exams through analytical reasoning.

By combining my engineering background with modern visual tools like **GeoGebra**, vector code-drawing languages like **FMSLogo**, and responsive mobile architectures like **Flutter**, I create software engines and interactive assets that turn static math formulas into living experiences.
