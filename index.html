About S. Rajarajan

A unique convergence of engineering expertise, software development, and mathematical storytelling.

Welcome! I am **S. Rajarajan**, a passionate educator, mobile developer, and control engineer on a mission to transform the way high school students understand and interact with mathematics.

Following a highly successful **37-year international career** in industrial automation across major sectors, I returned to my lifelong passion: teaching. Since 2016, I have designed tools and conducted specialized **one-day board prep revision seminars** to help Class 10 students overcome rote learning and achieve top marks through logic and visual reasoning.

By combining my engineering rigor with visual engines like **GeoGebra**, vector turtle-drawing in **FMSLogo**, and modern cross-platform development in **Flutter**, I build interactive pipelines that bring abstract geometry and formulas to life.
