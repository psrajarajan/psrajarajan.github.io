Divisibility Rule in Mathematics: X|Y (Y is divisible by X)

Created by S Rajarajan

This educational application helps us master mathematical divisibility rules through three interactive modules. We can practice identifying valid factors, explore the underlying mathematical concepts using any divisor of our choice, and test our knowledge with timed challenge sessions!
